# College Hostel Rules

## 1. Entry and Exit
- Students must follow the hostel entry and exit timings.
- Late entry requires permission from the hostel warden.

## 2. Cleanliness
- Students must keep their rooms clean.
- Common areas must be kept tidy.

## 3. Visitors
- Visitors must register at the hostel reception.
- Visitors must follow the hostel visiting hours.

## 4. Discipline
- Students must maintain discipline.
- Loud music and disturbing activities are not allowed.

## 5. Safety
- Students must follow hostel safety instructions.
- Any maintenance or safety issue should be reported to the hostel warden.

Note: These are sample rules created for the academic project.
